About EcoSort AI

Why we built this, and what makes it different.

🌍

Most waste ends up in the wrong bin for one simple reason: people don't always know which bin something belongs in. EcoSort AI removes that guesswork — point your camera at anything, and know instantly.

How it works

📸

Point

Show it anything through your camera or a photo — a bottle, a broken toy, a wrapper, whatever's actually in your hand.

🧠

Identify

AI recognises the object itself, not just a memorised checklist — so unusual or unfamiliar items still get identified.

♻️

Sort

Get the right bin instantly — spoken aloud in your language, with a disposal tip and eco-points for getting it right.

What makes it different

🔍

Recognises almost anything

Not limited to a small fixed list of trained objects — it can identify things it's never explicitly seen before.

🗣️

Speaks your language

English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu or Marathi — the whole site and every result, not just a translated label.

🌐

Works anywhere

A real, permanent website — reachable from any phone or laptop, any time, with nothing to install.

🔒

Your privacy, plainly

Sign in to keep your eco-points and history saved across visits. Browsing as a guest? Nothing is saved — ever.
